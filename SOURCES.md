## Why these sources shape the architecture

- Adaptive-analysis and multiple-testing work requires development search to be separated from confirmation and makes the comparison family part of the accepted study.
- ML reporting and artifact-review sources require more than a winning metric: study details, complete outcomes, compute, uncertainty, code/data rights and independent reproduction have separate evidence states.
- W3C PROV supports portable lineage, while Research Continuum adds research-specific edges such as `tests`, `supports`, `contradicts`, `evaluated_by` and `reproduces`.
- Hyperband is evidence that allocation algorithms are themselves testable policies under finite resources; it does not justify adopting an adaptive scheduler before simple baselines.
- NIST sources support explicit threat assumptions and protected environments, while leaving containment claims to actual platform tests.

## Before adopting a dependency, model or dataset

Record the exact official release and immutable locator, authorship, license/terms, maintenance and advisory state, runtime and transitive dependencies, safe-loading behavior, telemetry/network use, storage/compute cost, alternatives and rollback. A source being listed here does not authorize installation, data download, model use or cloud spend. Exact versions beyond the inspected baseline are deliberately deferred until an implementation environment and compatibility evidence exist.

For each dataset or model, document provenance, permitted use, attribution, redistribution, access conditions, generation method where applicable, geography/population/time coverage, transformations, deduplication and benchmark-contamination checks, uncertainty, missing variables and known harms. Link source records to all derived artifacts. Reject incompatible or unclear terms. Use a wholly synthetic fixture when it answers a contract question, label it honestly, and never relabel real/private records as synthetic.

Serialized models and data are executable-risk inputs until proven otherwise. Prefer non-executable formats and loaders configured to reject arbitrary remote code. Acquire inputs in a trusted preparation step, inspect them, record their exact identity and deny candidate-worker network by default.

## Citation and claim policy

Every source-derived statement in a `HypothesisRevision`, `StudyDesign` or `ResearchClaim` resolves to a `SourceRecord` and exact location when available. Record contradictory evidence and source limitations. Citation presence does not establish source quality or applicability; automated citation checks can reject missing/mismatched references but cannot replace scientific review.

The project's original content is AGPL-3.0-only. Referenced code, papers, datasets, models and standards retain their own terms. No third-party dataset, model weights or upstream implementation is bundled in this foundation candidate. A future public evidence package must include rights/redistribution status for every contained artifact and provide locators rather than copying material when redistribution is unavailable.
